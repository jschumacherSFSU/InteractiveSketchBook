Since I had already included if...else statements in sketch 4, I opted to try to figure out how to handle a couple of other scenarios that I ran into while working on Sketch 4. After Professor Oakley menitoned variables, I played with that idea. I looked at p5.js and the book resource and came up with the solution to use a boolean "showText" variable that I could set to false when I wanted the text to disappear. It took me a minute to figure out exact placement, but I managed to implement it successfully.
