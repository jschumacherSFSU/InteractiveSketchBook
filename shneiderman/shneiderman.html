Shneiderman's Eight Golden Rules

Which rule feels most absent from your micro-project page?

Without realizing it I designed my page for adults and didn't really consider a breadth of users, so rule #2 Seek universal usability is missing most from my sketchbook and microproject.

Which rule would most improve your sketchbook interface?

Rule #6 Permist easy reversal of actions would be the best addition to my sketchbook interface. Now there is only one direction - forward - for the user. They are not allowed to move forward or backward which if they changed their mind, they're forced to go through multiple unneccessary steps to just go back one screen or get directly to the menu anytime they want.

Which rule is hardest to implement honestly?

I think the hardest to implement is the universal usability. Finding the sweet spot for multiple users of varying ages and abilities seems qute challenging. Not that it can't be done, but there's a lot more thought required in the earlier stages to accommodate the multiple users.

Did any rule contradict how you designed your system originally?

I think that #7 would be the contradiction. I thought I was giving the users control, when actually, I was restricting them by not having enought options to allow them to navigate freely.
