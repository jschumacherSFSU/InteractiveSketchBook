Verb = prepare

I thought about preparation and it made me think of the steps needed to complete a task. This led me to building out circles, then I thought it might be more interesting to have different shapes so I added rectangles based on whether the value of "i" is odd or even. Then I thought what if I adjusted the color each time a shape was drawn to help signify the movement of the process, finishing with the common "green" signifying completion. I still get the x, y axes confused so it was a challenge to get the positioning of the shapes as I wanted them, but I got there eventually.

Regarding the reading, It was cool to see how much you can do with p5.js. I opted to just start with playing around with variables and if/else statements, mostly to adjust color. I needed to clean up my code to remove redundant code, but I ran out of time to implement anything else.
