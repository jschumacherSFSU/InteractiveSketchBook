UI/UX Reflection - Norman

Norman's Principles

Norman's principles of design emphasize the importance of user-centered design and the need to create interfaces that are intuitive and easy to use. His work highlights the significance of understanding user needs and behaviors in the design process.

Challenge

One of the key challenges in my microproject is ensuring that the design is accessible to all users, including those with disabilities. This aligns with Norman's principle of providing feedback and ensuring that users can easily understand how to interact with the interface.

Sketchbook Interface Audit

Upon reviewing my sketchbook interface, I realized that it lacks clear signifiers for interactive elements. This oversight can lead to confusion and frustration for users, as they may not easily identify how to navigate the interface.

Hardest Principle

The most challenging principle to implement is the idea of affordances. Creating visual cues that effectively communicate how elements can be interacted with requires careful consideration of design elements and user expectations.
