Micro Idea 3 Sketch

This is the preliminary sketch for MicroIdea 3.

This page will run a quiz that will help a user better understand their own vision and along the way learn about color blindness, the variations that exist, and possibly determine if they may have some level of deficiency. Then based on what their final outcome is, the screen color profile will change to better suit their vision needs.
